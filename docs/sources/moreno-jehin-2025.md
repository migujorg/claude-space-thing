# moreno-jehin-2025: Moreno & Jehin (2025): Monte Carlo dust tails of long-period comets (grain speeds)

- **Citation:** Moreno, F. & Jehin, E. (2025). Dust shells and dark linear structures on dust tails of historical and recent long-period comets. Astronomy & Astrophysics (arXiv:2503.10121).

## Contents

Dust-tail models of six long-period comets: β = C_pr Q_pr/(2ρr) with C_pr = 1.191e-3 kg m⁻², Q_pr ≈ 1 for r ≳ 1 µm; terminal speeds v = v0 β^γ r^Γ (cos z)^ε with γ = 0.25, Γ = −0.5 and v0 = 1 km/s for C/2023 A3.

## How it is used

Transcribed (dustEjection): grain speeds v0 β^0.25 r^−0.5 (zenith term averaged) set each tail packet's width (the shell of radius v·τ around its zero-velocity position) and, with Afρ = A Σ Q_σ/(2v), the cross-section production. C_pr agrees with the β from the solar luminosity within 4 %.

## Caveats

- v0 is fitted per comet; 1 km/s is C/2023 A3's.
