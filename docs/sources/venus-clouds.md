# Venus cloud tops and upper haze (`hansen-hovenier-1974`, `lee-2021-venus`, `pere-2016-venus`)

Transcribed: `pipeline/src/pipeline/photometry/tables/venus_clouds.json`. Code: `photometry/atmo_bodies.py`.

- **Hansen, J. E. & Hovenier, J. W. (1974).** Interpretation of the polarization of Venus. *J. Atmos. Sci.* 31, 1137–1160, DOI [10.1175/1520-0469(1974)031<1137:IOTPOV>2.0.CO;2](https://doi.org/10.1175/1520-0469(1974)031<1137:IOTPOV>2.0.CO;2). Abstract (AMS full-text page, saved by hand 2026-09-30 because the site refuses scripted clients; sha256 pinned): spherical particles with refractive index 1.46 / 1.44 / 1.43 (± 0.015) at 0.365 / 0.55 / 0.99 µm, effective radius 1.05 ± 0.10 µm, effective variance 0.07 ± 0.02, optical thickness unity near 50 mb; composition concentrated H2SO4–H2O.
- **Lee, Y. J., et al. (2021).** Investigation of UV absorbers on Venus using the 283 and 365 nm phase curves obtained from Akatsuki. *GRL* 48, e2020GL090577, DOI [10.1029/2020GL090577](https://doi.org/10.1029/2020GL090577); text arXiv:2103.09021v2 §3.2: cloud top (τ = 1 at 365 nm) at 70 km and a 4 km cloud scale height, "reasonably consistent with that used in previous studies (Haus et al., 2014; Ignatiev et al., 2009; Lee et al., 2012; Sato et al., 2020)"; §4.1: the unknown absorber must absorb within ~5 km below the cloud top.
- **Pere, C., et al. (2016).** Multilayer modeling of the aureole photometry during the Venus transit. *A&A* 595, A115, DOI [10.1051/0004-6361/201628528](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201628528); text arXiv:1608.08544v1, abstract: aerosol scale height 4.8 ± 0.5 km in the upper haze above 80 km (2012 transit, SDO/HMI with VEx/SOIR).

**Use:** `atmospheres.json` Venus `cloud` component, 60–110 km (label **estimated**): τ_365(z) = e^(−(z−70)/4) to 80 km, then a 4.8 km scale height; spectral shape and phase function from Mie theory (Bohren & Huffman BHMIE, `atmo.py`) for a gamma size distribution with H&H's r_eff and v_eff, their real refractive index and k = 0 (the gamma form and k = 0 are the assumptions). Below 60 km the deck is opaque: its appearance is the photometric model of `photometry.json`. Mie g(550) = 0.72.

**Not accessible:** Ignatiev et al. (2009, JGR) and Haus et al. (2014, Icarus), the primary cloud-top altimetry and structure papers (Wiley / Elsevier); the Venus Express/VIRA-2 review Titov et al. (2018, *Space Sci. Rev.*) is open access but Springer serves scripted clients a JavaScript challenge.
