# Grav et al. (2015) irregular satellites of Jupiter and Saturn (`grav-2015`) — transcription

**Citation:** Grav, T., Bauer, J. M., Mainzer, A. K., Masiero, J. R., Nugent, C. R., Cutri, R. M., Sonnett, S. & Kramer, E. (2015). NEOWISE: observations of the irregular satellites of Jupiter and Saturn. *Astrophysical Journal* 809, 3. DOI [10.1088/0004-637X/809/1/3](https://doi.org/10.1088/0004-637X/809/1/3). arXiv:1505.07820v1 (fetched, sha256 recorded).

**Transcribed** (`tables/grav_2015_irregulars.json`), for the irregular satellites that have pck00011 radii (Himalia, Elara, Pasiphae, Sinope, Lysithea, Carme, Ananke, Leda, Phoebe):
- **Table 1** (p. 4): V absolute magnitude H and IAU slope parameter G. The authors compiled these from Luu (1991), Rettig et al. (2001), Grav et al. (2003), Grav & Bauer (2007) and Bauer et al. (2006). For every Jovian satellite G = 0.10 ± 0.15 is *assumed* (caption); Phoebe's G = 0.02 ± 0.033.
- **Table 3** (p. 7): NEOWISE thermal-model diameter and visible albedo. For Phoebe we take the first of the two rows, the fit discussed in Sec. 4.12.

**Use and caveats:**
- **Brightness:** p = 10^(−0.4(H − V☉))/(R/AU)² at the pck00011 radius R, so V(1,0) = H exactly. `geometricAlbedoV` is **derived**.
- **Colour:** **estimated**, with p ASSUMED grey. The measured broadband colours (Grav et al. 2003; Rettig et al. 2001) are printed as an image in the arXiv version and were not transcribed.
- **Radius:** the pck00011 radii of the small Jovian irregulars are round numbers (e.g. Leda 5 km, whereas NEOWISE gives D = 21.5 km). The albedo at the pck radius therefore differs from the physical one (Leda 0.157 here against NEOWISE's 0.034) while the brightness is the same. The method text gives both.
- **Phase function:** IAU H–G with the tabulated G, over 0–12° (Jupiter) or 0–6.5° (Saturn), the phase ranges seen from Earth. Label **estimated**, since G is assumed for the Jovian satellites.
- **Not represented:** rotational lightcurves of about 0.2 mag (Himalia, Phoebe; paper Sec. 4).
