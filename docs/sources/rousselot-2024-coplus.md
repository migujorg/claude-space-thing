## Contents

Fluorescence model of CO⁺ A²Π–X²Σ⁺: efficiencies of the (v′, v″) bands v′, v″ = 0–5 at r = 1 au (Table 1, erg s⁻¹ ion⁻¹) and wavelengths of lines of the (3,0) and (2,0) bands (Table 4).

## How it is used

Transcribed (coPlus): each CO⁺ ion radiates the sum of Table 1 (9.5e-14 erg s⁻¹ at 1 au, × r⁻²), placed at the (3,0) and (2,0) bands (402 and 427 nm) in proportion to their efficiencies — the ion tail's colour.

## Caveats

- Only two bands have wavelengths in the source; the others (3800–5000 Å) are folded into them.
