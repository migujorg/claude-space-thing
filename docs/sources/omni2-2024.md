## Contents

One record per hour of 2024: interplanetary magnetic field and plasma parameters at 1 au; word 25 is the flow speed (km/s), 9999 when missing (omni2.text).

## How it is used

`comets/model.json` solarWind: the median of the 8581 valid hourly speeds, 385 km/s (16–84 %: 334–458 km/s), for the ion tail's aberration (the tail lies along v_sw r̂ − v_comet) and the ions' speed along it.

## Caveats

- Near the ecliptic at 1 au during solar maximum; comets at high heliographic latitude see the fast wind (~750 km/s) near solar minimum.
- The wind is gusty: real ion tails kink and disconnect; a single median speed draws a straight tail.
