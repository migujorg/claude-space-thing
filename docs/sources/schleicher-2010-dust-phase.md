## Contents

Phase function of cometary dust, 0–180° in 1° steps, normalised at 0° and at 90°: the Halley curve of Schleicher et al. (1998) at small phase angles spliced with Marcus's (2007) Henyey–Greenstein curve at large ones.

## How it is used

Dust-tail packets are scaled by Φ(α_packet)/Φ(α_nucleus): the coma's Afρ is the one seen at the nucleus' phase angle, a packet elsewhere in the tail is seen at its own.

## Caveats

- A composite of two comets' behaviour; individual comets differ, notably at large phase (forward scattering).
