## What is used

The earlier completeness statement for Uranus is recorded with the newer one (`sheppard-2024-uranus-neptune`), which supersedes it. The survey had a 50 % detection efficiency at m_R = 26.1, about 7 km in radius for albedo 0.04, over most of the Hill sphere (abstract). The size distribution, q = 1.8 ± 0.3, is measured only for radii > 7 km, where the survey is complete (Sec. 4.1). So no slope below the limit is published, and the layer adds no Uranian irregular moons.
