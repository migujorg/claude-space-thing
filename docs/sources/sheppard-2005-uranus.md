## What is used

The earlier completeness statement for Uranus is recorded with the newer one (`sheppard-2024-uranus-neptune`), which supersedes it. The survey had a 50 % detection efficiency at m_R = 26.1, about 7 km in radius for albedo 0.04, over most of the Hill sphere (abstract). The size distribution, q = 1.8 ± 0.3, is measured only for radii > 7 km, where the survey is complete (Sec. 4.1). So no supported slope below the limit was found in the sources reviewed, and the layer adds no Uranian irregular moons.

Product-use scope: catalogue-count conditioning and a fitted H proxy do not establish detection probability for a generated orbit or guarantee consistency with all observations. Discovery yield is aggregate under fixed inputs; catalogue refits can change counts and identities. Source survey efficiencies and completeness statements above retain their published domains; the current generator does not apply their pointings or efficiencies as an object veto.
