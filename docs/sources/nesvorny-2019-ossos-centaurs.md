## What is used

- **The orbital model** behind the Kurlander et al. (2025) archive (`kurlander-2025-centaurs`). The archive, not this paper, supplies the orbits.
- **A cross-check of the normalization** (abstract): "The population of Centaurs is estimated to be 21,000 ± 8,000 for D > 10 km" (H_r < 13.7 for a 6 % albedo; a < 30 au, q > 7.5 au). The stage uses Kurlander et al.'s 21 400 (+3 400/−2 800), which is consistent with it and better constrained.
