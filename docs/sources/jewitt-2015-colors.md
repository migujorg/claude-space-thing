## Contents

BVRI photometry of active comae; mean colours of 26 long-period comets B−V = 0.78±0.02, V−R = 0.47±0.02, R−I = 0.42±0.03; of active short-period comets (Solontoi et al. 2012) B−V = 0.80±0.01, V−R = 0.46±0.01; the Sun B−V = 0.64, V−R = 0.35, R−I = 0.33 (Table 3).

## How it is used

Transcribed (dustColour). The colour excesses over the Sun become a piecewise-linear reflectance through the Bessell B, V, R, I effective wavelengths (normalised at 484.5 nm, the Afρ continuum); sunlight × reflectance integrated against the CIE observers is the dust continuum's XYZS. Normalised gradient ≈ 12 %/100 nm.

## Caveats

- Measured in apertures of a few arcsec, mostly of distant comets where gas contributes little; taken as the colour of the dust. Short-period comets have no R−I in the source (the long-period excess is used).
