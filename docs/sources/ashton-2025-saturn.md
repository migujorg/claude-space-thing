## What is used

- **The band of the MPC's H** (Sec. 4.1): "We will use the V-band filter for our size calculations since that is the filter the Minor Planet Center uses for their values for H of irregular moons". The stage therefore reads the MPC natural-satellite H as H_V (`mpc-natsats`).
- **Context, not a number.** The sample is not debiased (Sec. 5.2), so it gives no population model. Its discoveries are in the MPC list the stage conditions on. The later 128 moons are in the same list (Ashton et al. 2025, RNAAS 9, 57, DOI:10.3847/2515-5172/adbf87; a discovery note with no population estimate).
