## What is used (transcribed in `syn_tables/populations.json`, `irregularMoons.uranus/neptune`)

- **Completeness** (Sec. 2.1–2.2, abstract): "The outer satellites of Uranus should be nearly complete to about 26.5 mags, corresponding to satellites of about 8 km in diameter", and those of Neptune "to about 27 mags, … about 14 km" (r band, albedo 0.1). On the MPC scale, through the discovery magnitudes of S/2023 U1 (26.6), S/2021 N1 (26.9) and S/2002 N5 (25.9), these are H_V ≈ 13.6 and 12.3.
- **No population below the limit.** The paper reports discoveries and expects "many more smaller satellites … but current surveys have not gone deep enough" (Sec. 5). It gives no debiased number. The layer therefore adds no Uranian or Neptunian moons.
- **Statements for Jupiter and Saturn** (Sec. 1, citing Sheppard et al. 2023): "Jupiter's outer satellites are complete to about 2 km and Saturn to about 3 km". These are compared with the limits the stage finds (docs/reports/synthetic-populations.md §12.2) but not used as numbers.
