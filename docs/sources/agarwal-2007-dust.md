## Contents

Review of dust dynamics for comet 67P: β = 3L Q_pr/(16π c G M ρ s) (Eq. 9); in-situ size distributions (Table 1: 1P/Halley DIDSY > 20 µm index −3.5 ± 0.2; Stardust samples to 1e-3 kg); syndynes and synchrones and the finite width v·τ of a grain shell; bulk density 1000 kg m⁻³ in its coma model.

## How it is used

Transcribed (dustGrains): size index −3.5, density 1000 kg m⁻³, largest grain from 1e-3 kg (6.2 mm); β from the formula with the IAU solar luminosity, GM_sun and c: 0.574 at 1 µm down to 9.3e-5 at 6.2 mm.

## Caveats

- The size index is an in-situ value for Halley; tail models of other comets find −3 to −4.
