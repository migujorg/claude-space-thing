## Contents

Review; Sect. 2.2: Q_CO/Q_H2O from < 1 % (C/2000 WM1) to > 20 % (C/1996 B2), varying by a factor of 50 across comets observed in the UV.

## How it is used

Transcribed (coAbundance): the ion tail uses the geometric mean of 1 % and 20 % (4.5 %).

## Caveats

- An individual comet can be 5× brighter or fainter in CO⁺; the ion tail's brightness is estimated.
