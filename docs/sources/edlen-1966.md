# Edlén (1966) refractive index of standard air (`edlen-1966`)

**Citation:** Edlén, B. (1966). The refractive index of air. *Metrologia* 2, 71–80. DOI [10.1088/0026-1394/2/2/002](https://doi.org/10.1088/0026-1394/2/2/002).

**Used:** Eq. 1 for standard air (dry, 15 °C, 101 325 Pa, 0.03 % CO₂), which is the "standard air" in which the CIE tables are defined:

(n − 1)·10⁸ = 8342.13 + 2 406 030/(130 − σ²) + 15 997/(38.9 − σ²), σ = vacuum wavenumber in µm⁻¹.

Converts the TSIS-1 HSRS vacuum wavelengths to air (λ_air = λ_vac/n; 0.153 nm at 550 nm) with the per-nm density multiplied by dλ_vac/dλ_air. A formula, not a dataset: the SourceRecord has no sha256.
