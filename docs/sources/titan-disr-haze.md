# Titan's atmosphere: DISR haze model and HASI profile (`bazzon-2014`, `hasi-l4-descent`, `hasi-l4-entry`, `peck-khanna-1966-n2`)

## Haze extinction (Huygens DISR)
- **Primary:** Tomasko, M. G., Doose, L., Engel, S., et al. (2008). A model of Titan's aerosols based on measurements made inside the atmosphere. *Planetary and Space Science* 56, 669–707, DOI [10.1016/j.pss.2007.11.019](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pss.2007.11.019). **Not accessible to this pipeline** (Elsevier; no open copy found — OpenAlex lists it as closed access). The same holds for Doose et al. (2016), *Icarus* 270, 355, DOI [10.1016/j.icarus.2015.09.039](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.icarus.2015.09.039).
- **Transcription used:** Bazzon, A., Schmid, H. M. & Buenzli, E. (2014). HST observations of the limb polarization of Titan. *A&A* 572, A6, DOI [10.1051/0004-6361/201323139](https://doi.org/10.1051/0004-6361/201323139); text arXiv:1409.3421v1, Appendix A.4 (p. 13), eqs. A.8–A.13, read from a 200 dpi rendering of the page. They transcribe Tomasko et al.'s Fig. 47: cumulative haze optical depth from the top, τ80(λ) = 1.012e7 λ^-2.339 above 80 km with a 65 km scale height, + τ30(λ) = 2.029e4 λ^-1.409 linear over 30–80 km, + τ0(λ) = 6.270e2 λ^-0.9706 linear below 30 km (λ in nm; valid 400–1600 nm per their §7.1). Stored in `pipeline/src/pipeline/photometry/tables/titan_disr_haze.json`.
- **Cross-check:** Vincendon & Langevin (2010, arXiv:1103.3215 §3.1) quote Tomasko et al.'s total optical depth at 1.08 µm as 2.6 and exponents 0.97 (below 30 km) and 1.41 (30–80 km); the transcription gives 2.61 at 1.08 µm and the same two exponents. Their third exponent, "2.43 above 80 km", differs from Bazzon's 2.339; with 2.43 (and the same coefficient) the 1.08 µm total would be 2.2 instead of their own 2.6, so Bazzon's 2.339 is kept and 2.43 is taken to be a transposition.
- **Use:** `atmospheres.json` Titan `haze` extinction (label **estimated**: DISR measured from ~150 km to the surface at one site and time; above 150 km the 65 km exponential is extrapolated; 360–400 nm is a power-law extrapolation). τ(550 nm) = 8.1 from the top to the surface.
- **Unknown:** the haze single-scattering albedo (Tomasko et al. Table 2, Fig. 48; Bazzon et al. only say "typically 0.8–1") and phase functions (Tomasko et al.'s tables for above and below 80 km). Needed to render Titan's haze; supply a copy of Tomasko et al. (2008) to fill them.

## Gas profile (Huygens HASI)
- **Data:** NASA PDS Atmospheres Node, data set HP-SSA-HASI-2-3-4-MISSION-V1.1 (`hphasi_0001/DATA/PROFILES/`): `HASI_L4_ATMO_PROFILE_DESCEN.TAB` (time, altitude m, pressure Pa, temperature K, density; 146.8 km to the surface) and `HASI_L4_ATMO_PROFILE_ENTRY.TAB` (1380–157 km; pressure, temperature). Fulchignoni, M., et al. (2005), *Nature* 438, 785–791, DOI [10.1038/nature04314](https://doi.org/10.1038/nature04314).
- **Use:** n = P/(kT), log-linear in altitude (bridging 147–157 km); surface 146 645 Pa, 93.50 K. N2 Rayleigh with the Peck & Khanna (1966) refractive index (refractiveindex-info.md) and Bodhaine's F(N2): τ(550 nm) = 1.09. Methane (1.5–5 %) is counted as N2 (estimate).
