## Contents

A chemistry–emission model of the [O I] 5577, 6300, 6364 Å lines in comet Hale-Bopp: photorates, branching ratios and yields (Tables 1 and 4, Sect. 4).

## How it is used

Transcribed (oxygenRedDoublet): O(¹D) per water molecule 0.064 (direct) + 0.81 × 0.357 (via OH) = 0.353 photons, 75 % at 6300 Å and 25 % at 6364 Å.

## Caveats

- Collisional quenching of O(¹D) in the dense inner coma is neglected (an upper bound for very productive comets).
